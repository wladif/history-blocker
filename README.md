# History Blocker

### Summary
Automatically removes browsing history entries from user-blacklisted domains, keeping your browser history clean and private.


### Desciption
Keep your browsing history clean and private with an extension that automatically removes visits to websites you've blacklisted. Simply add domains to your blacklist, and the extension will automatically detect and delete matching entries from your browser history. No more manually clearing unwanted browsing records or worrying about leaving traces of specific websites. You stay in control of what gets saved in your browsing history.
